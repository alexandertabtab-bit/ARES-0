# Safety, Ethics, and Limitations

ARES is an early-stage student prototype. It is not certified equipment and must not be used as an operational emergency system.

## Operational limits
- A drone is not a replacement for firefighters, evacuation plans, fixed fire-protection systems, or trained incident commanders.
- Drone flight near heat, smoke, strong airflow, chemicals, or industrial equipment requires specialist assessment.
- Ordinary drones must not enter potentially explosive atmospheres unless the equipment and operating procedure are appropriately certified.
- Payload delivery or fire suppression requires engineering validation and must be suitable for the specific fire class and site procedure.
- Water must not be assumed safe for energized electrical equipment or every chemical incident.
- Thermal cameras have limitations and cannot see through arbitrary walls or dense smoke.

## AI limits
- A visual model can produce false positives and false negatives.
- Visible smoke/fire detection is not chemical identification.
- Sensor anomalies are not proof of fire.
- The prototype risk score is illustrative, not a validated probability.
- Human operators must verify alerts using approved procedures.

## Privacy and data
- Avoid recording identifiable people unless necessary and authorized.
- Protect facility layouts, chemical inventories, and operational data.
- Use datasets only when their licenses permit the intended use.

## Human responsibility
All evacuation, isolation, shutdown, suppression, and entry decisions remain with qualified personnel and established emergency procedures.
